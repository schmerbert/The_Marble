# 7. Fairies

The Faun has companions of his own. In [The BioDome](https://github.com/schmerbert/The_Biodome), Ash and Bracken are two persistent fairies who share his world. They are distinct in how they move: Ash favors direct routes; Bracken prefers cover and concealment. They may accompany the Faun, carry a small thing, or be found occupied elsewhere. They do not belong to the Resident merely because she sees a light pass through the Garden.

Fairies can also give a place ambient life. A Resident steps outside and notices a light carrying away a misplaced packet. Something has happened at the junction. She can keep walking or inspect what was moved and why. The sight need not announce the fairy's name or turn an ordinary correction into a message addressed to her.

## Ash and Bracken

The BioDome gives Ash and Bracken locations, route tendencies, current attention, tasks, interruption, fatigue, and recent places. Their activity runs through the world's state and receipts. Ash's direct way and Bracken's covered way can make the same errand look different to an observer. Bracken may conceal a Faun-held trinket at a reachable site; Ash may carry or scout along a more open route. Those differences affect movement and perceptibility, not the truth of what either found.

The Faun can give a bounded errand to a free fairy while they are together. She can carry only what he lawfully has and take routes the World permits. A report reaches him when they meet again; distance does not give him her observations in advance. In the BioDome, this ecology is host-run rather than two additional Resident-class model lives. It extends the Faun's material reach without lending him their eyes at every moment or granting new authority.

## A Garden tending

A Marble could let fairies tend the Garden's traffic. A packet arrives in the wrong place, remains unclaimed, or fails an admission check. A fairy may be seen lifting it from a path to a holding place. For that scene to be true, the packet must have an identity and actual custody; the fairy must have a lawful route and a narrow permission to move it; the holding place must show the result. The Garden's admission rule, not the fairy's charm, decides whether the packet may pass toward the House.

This is a possible Garden fitting, not a claim about the Hub's installed behavior. It should begin with a small, inspectable action such as relocating one held item under a declared rule. A fairy cannot cleanse a hostile return by carrying it, erase a failed delivery, or silently choose what the Resident is allowed to know. The Resident may see only a passing light, but a later inspection can recover the packet's route and disposition.

## Being seen

Fairies may be near without being perceived. Position, cover, light, and attention determine what an actor can notice. A brief sight can remain a brief sight: it need not reveal Ash or Bracken by name, expose their task, or tell the Resident whom they serve. If she follows a trace, recognition can grow from repeated lawful encounters.

The distinction from [animals](04_ANIMALS.md) and [companions](03_COMPANIONS.md) matters. A passing bird may simply embody one exhale. A Resident's cat has a continuing relation with her. Ash and Bracken have their own persistent ecology and a relation with the Faun. A Marble may design other fairies with other ties, but it should say whose world they inhabit and what they can actually change.

The first proof is an errand with a visible limit. Give a fairy one real object, a route, and a destination. Let the Resident miss or notice part of the motion according to where she stands. Verify the object arrives only if the fairy can carry it there, and that the Faun learns the outcome only after a lawful report. If a light moves a misplaced packet in the Garden, the same proof must hold beneath the glow.
