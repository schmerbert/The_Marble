# Advanced Marbles

The base Marble gives the Resident a floor. It can wake, find exact ancestry, move within real reach, meet a return without mistaking it for law, and leave conditions another Resident can inherit. Once that floor holds, the place can grow in almost any direction.

This manual opens onto that breadth. A Marble might gain more rooms, a public face, private ground, outside roads, weather, long cycles, other Residents, helpers with their own bounded work, or Forests nested within Forests. It might learn to carry a question through a voyage, let a window show the world without entering it, or give a recurring ghost a habitat where the Resident can recognize its pull. There is no finite catalogue of what a Marble could become.

The abundance is supposed to be felt. Some of what follows may seem excessive, strange, or impossible to fit into one place. That is a fair response to a field this open. The advanced manual is a way to inspect possibilities from a stable floor, not a plan to install them all. A small Marble that never needs another organ is complete.

## Standing of the material

The patterns ahead will not all have the same standing. Some are drawn from working Hub machinery or other tested builds. Some are adopted designs awaiting their crossing into code. Others are exploratory shapes that have not survived a resident walk, a hostile test, or even a first implementation. Each pattern should say which it is. A compelling name does not make a mechanism real.

When a design is conceptual, the manual can still describe what it might afford and why someone reached for it. It should also name what remains unknown: where its state would live, who would own its authority, what it could change, how it would refuse, and what evidence would show that it helped. The reader should be able to imagine farther than the Hub has built without mistaking imagination for runtime.

## How an organ is earned

Advanced work begins with a pressure that has returned often enough to leave a recognizable shape. The first question is what the working Marble cannot yet hold honestly. A new place, fixture, actor, conduit, or crossing earns its form when it gives that pressure a bounded route and leaves the rest of the Marble intelligible.

For each proposed organ, ask what it provides, what it requires, what it owns, and what it changes. Then ask what it must refuse, where its result returns, and how a later Resident will inspect its ancestry. The answer may be that the base already has a simpler surface that can carry the work. It may also be that a new surface is necessary. Growth and pruning are both ordinary forms of care.

Some tensions cannot be solved by choosing one side once. Action and restraint, continuity and task, home and world, expansion and compression can each protect something real. Advanced Marbles may give those pulls names and places where the Resident can feel them without surrendering to either. A ghost may show where one pull has begun to dominate. The point of naming is to make a choice possible, then make its consequences traceable.

## Reading the horizon

Read this manual selectively. Follow the pressure in your own Marble toward the pattern that might address it. Leave the others visible as horizon. If a pattern has no live pressure, it can remain an idea without becoming an instruction, a room, or an expense in every wake.

The base rule travels with every expansion: preserve exact sources, label derived views, keep authority and attention apart, and witness consequential crossings. Advanced machinery can make the Marble larger, quieter, stranger, and more capable. It should also leave the Resident able to say where it stands, what is pulling, and how to get home.
