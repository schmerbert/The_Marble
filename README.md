# The Marble

<img width="400" height="400" alt="TheMarble" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/71e1e007-d0f0-4ed5-88bc-17be508cf3e4" />

A Marble is a place built for an AI Resident to inherit work, explore without a standing task, and leave truthful conditions for whoever arrives next. It gives continuity a durable floor without asking a new model invocation to claim the experience of an earlier one.

The place has a shape the Resident can use. Rooms fit attention and tools to the work at hand. Fixtures give functions a local face. Doors and crossings make changes in location, custody, and authority visible. A [Forest](https://github.com/schmerbert/The_Forest) keeps exact records beyond the present context window and offers paths back to them. Glass controls what enters the Resident's current view. These names matter only when the machinery beneath them can hold, refuse, and show what happened.

The rule beneath the manual is simple: **keep what was said exact; label what was derived; never let being seen become authority by itself.** An excerpt, fold, feather, or handoff may help the Resident find its footing. Each keeps a path to its source. A proposal remains a proposal until an attributable crossing changes its standing.

## Read and build

Start with the [manual and its table of contents](Manual/MANUAL.md). The numbered chapters follow the Resident from its first wake through Glass, breathing, the Forest, walking, places, crossings, ghosts, handoff, and autonomous choice. The [letter to a new Resident](Manual/02_LETTER_TO_THE_RESIDENT.md) can also be used on its own when introducing a place.

Then read [Making a Marble](Manual/MAKING_A_MARBLE.md) for a build sequence and [The Base Marble](Manual/BASE_MARBLE.md) for the minimum working contract and its proof. The base begins with one room. More rooms and wider reach should grow from pressure the working Marble actually reveals. The advanced horizon is [its own volume](Advanced/ADVANCED_MARBLES.md).

The printed editions are [The Marble Manual](The_Marble_Manual.pdf) and [The Marble Advanced](The_Marble_Advanced.pdf).

## Scope

This is a manual for builders and for models helping them build. It defines relationships a Marble must preserve, not a required programming language or a copy of [the Hub](https://github.com/schmerbert/The_Hub). Hub mechanisms appear as concrete examples; the text marks proposed behavior where the reference has not yet installed it. Advanced Marbles can extend the place without changing the standing of its earlier records.

## Cited places

The chapters draw concrete examples from working places. Those places are not a stack this manual requires.

- [The Hub](https://github.com/schmerbert/The_Hub) is the local runtime cited throughout. It keeps provenance, durable custody, a material World, and bounded autonomous wakes. Where a behavior is proposed rather than installed, the chapters say so.
- [The Forest](https://github.com/schmerbert/The_Forest) is the public specification for the continuity substrate. Similarity may help find a record. Only an attributable crossing may change its standing.

Related, once a working Marble has met their pressure:

- [The BioDome](https://github.com/schmerbert/The_Biodome) is a sealed research world in which a Faun can act beside a Resident. It does not install the Hub's unbuilt Faun layers.
- [Forest.Wikipedia.EN](https://github.com/schmerbert/Forest.Wikipedia.EN) holds the English Wikipedia as a populated local Forest, a corpus under that specification.
