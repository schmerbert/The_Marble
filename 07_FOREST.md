# The Forest

The Glass returns only what fits the present. Behind a short return, the Marble needs somewhere to keep the records it did not show and the relationships that make them findable again. The Forest is that continuity terrain.

It reaches across wakes and sessions. A record left long before the current conversation can still be encountered with its source, author, time, and authority visible. The Resident need not carry the entire archive in context to know that it exists or to seek a particular part of it.

## Records and lines

The Forest holds records with their bodies and provenance. A record may be a human or Resident utterance, a deliberate Journal entry, an outside source, or a separately attributed synthesis. Its presence answers **what was kept**. It does not answer **what is true**.

Lines between records have distinct meanings. Conversation links put utterances in sequence; the Hub keeps each utterance as its own record. Provenance links show where material came from or what a later record drew upon. A citation is different from a resemblance. The Forest must preserve those differences even when all are represented as edges in a graph.

The underlying Source and Scroll keep the exact causal account, including actions and returns that are not ordinary Forest leaves. The Forest provides a traversable projection of that ancestry. It should lead back to its sources rather than becoming a replacement account of them.

## Home, Wild, and Mycelium

**Home** holds material made within the Marble's own work and conversation: known terrain with attributable authors. Home is not a synonym for true.

**Wild** holds material brought from outside. Its presence and source can be known while its fit with local ground remains unresolved. Wild is not a synonym for false.

**Mycelium** names the possibility of questions that arise between records: a connection the Resident had not thought to request. It may surface an adjacent inquiry without inventing an answer or planting durable belief. A Forest can be useful without Mycelium; when it is fitted, its questions must carry the uncertainty of their origin.

These are not degrees of authority. Origin, kind, and authority remain separate. A Home inference can be wrong; a Wild source can be sound; a fruitful question can remain unanswered.

## Ground and growth

Nothing becomes ground merely because it was stored, recalled, read, or visited often. Promotion is a separate, attributable crossing. Correction should supersede accepted ground without erasing the record of what was accepted before.

The Forest can grow without forcing every new record into present attention. It can also acquire paths from walks actually taken. A proposed connection and a witnessed step are different records; repeated steps may give a path wear without making its destination true. The next chapter describes how the Resident walks this terrain and returns.

The Hub and the public [Forest reference](https://github.com/schmerbert/The_Forest) provide working forms of this substrate. Their schemas and names are examples of how to keep these relationships exact, not a required database design for every Marble.
