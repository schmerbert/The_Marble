# Crossings

A thing may be present without being permitted to take root. The Resident can read an outside page, consider a proposal, or see a feather from the Forest. Each encounter may matter. None by itself makes the page local ground, the proposal a decision, or the feather a new memory.

A **crossing** is the declared point where standing or state changes. It can admit a record, move the Resident, alter a fixture, commit a decision, or carry a result into wider reach. The crossing names what changed and refuses to let a neighboring action impersonate it.

## Custody, attention, authority

Custody answers what was kept. Attention answers what the Resident sees now. Authority answers what the material may govern. State answers what actually changed. A source may be in custody and out of attention. An exhale may enter attention without gaining authority. A successful action may change state without making every explanation in its return true.

The builder should choose the dangerous crossing in a small Marble first. A fluent Resident proposal may look like an accepted human decision. An outside source may look like a fact the House already knows. A shortened exchange may look like the words originally spoken. The crossing that prevents that promotion must have a real refusal path.

## The contract

For each consequential crossing, specify:

1. **Source:** the exact actor or material arriving, with an attributable pointer.
2. **Destination:** the state, place, custody, or authority it seeks.
3. **Owner:** who or what is allowed to decide this change.
4. **Conditions:** required standing, scope, freshness, consent, and conflict checks.
5. **Treatment:** what may be selected, cleaned, or omitted during passage.
6. **Effect:** the one state change success means, including what it does *not* change.
7. **Witness:** what lets a later Resident or builder verify success or refusal.

An identifier is not permission. A passage in the map is not proof it was traversed. A tool call is an attempt until the governing state and its witness say what happened. A pleasant return cannot turn refusal into success.

## A package in the Garden

Imagine a package arriving from the Road. The Garden can receive and hold it. That is one crossing: outside material now has a named, inspectable position at the junction. Opening it for inspection is another. Admitting its contents into the House, planting an excerpt in the Forest, and accepting a claim from it as ground are further crossings with different owners and conditions. A single `received` flag cannot honestly stand for all of them.

The Road delivery and iron gate remain future fittings in the Hub. The example describes the law they would need; their names do not install them. In a smaller Marble, the same distinction can be tested with one outside document and one explicit admission action.

## The Bear at the Wild crossing

Home and Wild need a threshold the Resident can feel. Before the Resident enters Wild terrain, the Bear asks why it is going. The answer is an exact, attributable seeking: a question or purpose that gives the journey its first bearing. A valid reason need not predict the answer, but it must be specific enough to guide entry and inspect what the journey later found. If the Resident cannot name one, the crossing may refuse and leave it in Home. A vague appetite for more material is not automatically a bearing.

The Bear does not decide what is true in Wild. It checks the reason for crossing and helps derive a navigational query from that reason. The exact words of the seeking remain available beside the derived bearing. The Resident may reorient as it learns; each changed bearing should remain attributable to the journey rather than silently replacing its purpose.

The homeward boundary is stricter. Raw Wild packets do not pass into Home as though encounter made them the Resident's own words. Before return, the Resident makes an explicit synthesis of the journey. It may speak an utterance, write a Journal entry, or state that it found nothing it can responsibly carry. Thinking prepares that act; the committed speech or writing is its visible crossing. The synthesis names its Wild sources and uncertainty where they matter. It becomes a new Resident-authored Home record, while the Wild material keeps its own origin and standing.

This is the intended Forest law, beyond the Hub's current implementation. The Hub can admit typed outside material to Wild and exact Resident utterances or deliberate Journal entries to Home. Its Bear/Faun entry question, traversable Wild expedition, and required homeward synthesis are not installed. Earlier Hub design also permits a bare `Leave` with no Home synthesis; this manual states the stronger target for a Marble built to this rule.

## Airlocks

Some crossings need time on both sides of the threshold. Suppose the Resident goes down the Road into the web. Before departure, an airlock records why it is going, what it may reach, what it may carry, and what action would require a separate approval. This prepares an expedition; it does not grant every possible act outside.

On return, the airlock stops the passage while what is *on board* is examined. The host identifies exact sources and results, checks their integrity and scope, treats hostile instructions as outside text rather than commands, and separates useful material from the unnecessary or suspect. It may admit, restrict, quarantine, or refuse each item. A safe-to-hold result is not thereby true or Home. Exact outside returns needed later can enter Wild with their source and crossing receipts.

Only after that inspection may the Resident carry an authored synthesis toward Home. It can say what it learned, what remains doubtful, and which Wild records support its account. Speaking or writing this synthesis is a new attributable act; it does not overwrite the exact results. If nothing is fit to carry, the Resident can say that. The airlock's completed return should witness both what was admitted and what was left behind.

The same pattern can serve any boundary where a journey may gather material that must be checked before reentry. The Hub's Road, Iron Gate, staged airlocks, and quarantine remain proposed rather than installed. Its current Source, Scrub, and typed Wild intake provide narrower working pieces, not a completed outside expedition protocol.

## Refusal leaves ground

A boundary that only admits is a hole. A boundary that only refuses leaves no way to live. The valid case should pass under the same law that stops the tempting invalid one.

When a crossing refuses, the source remains what it was. The Resident should learn what failed and where it still stands. When it succeeds, the destination should show the change, and a durable witness should bind the result to the request and source. If the host crashes between decision and effect, the next arrival must be able to distinguish an unfinished attempt from a committed change.

This is where the Marble's metaphors become enforceable. A door holds because World state agrees that it opened and that the Resident crossed. A Garden protects because material can be held there without being admitted farther. A Forest remains usable because being encountered, followed, and planted are different acts.
