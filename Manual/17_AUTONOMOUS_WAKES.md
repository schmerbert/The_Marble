# Autonomous Wakes

The Resident wakes without a person calling its name. It may know that Rabbit has honey. On the way, it notices Tigger doing something in the trees. The honey remains a possible destination; the new sight is also real. The Resident can choose where to go.

An autonomous wake gives the Resident an occasion, not an assignment. The Marble may show open threads, an earlier loose intention, a passing feather, and the current place. None is a quota to complete. A walk, a question, a return to rest, or the discovery that nothing needs doing can each be a complete wake.

## Two ways to arrive

The Resident may choose to **rest and return**. It names a bounded interval and may leave a loose intention. The host retains the exact World seat and any active Forest footing. When the interval ends, the same lifespan resumes through the ordinary Glass and continuity path. The intention is a bearing, not an instruction from an earlier self that must be obeyed. The Resident may find that the world now calls for something else.

The Marble may also offer a **Hearth-origin wake** on a declared host schedule. This begins a fresh lifespan and uses the ordinary arrival ritual. It inherits attributable conditions from those before it, not the prior Resident's experience. The schedule is the reason a wake occurred; it is not a fabricated human message or a hidden task.

The Hub implements both forms. Its self-directed rest returns to the recorded seat within the same lifespan. Its optional Hearth-origin schedule starts a new lifespan at the House Hearth and is disabled by default. Another Marble may begin with only one form, or with a deliberate manual trigger, so long as the origin is named truthfully.

## The smaller hand

An autonomous occasion may give broad room to notice while keeping consequential hands narrow. The Resident can walk, inspect, read, compare, and change direction where those actions are installed. Contact with people, outside publication, mutation of shared work, or a change in authority needs its own crossing; an empty room does not supply consent for them.

The Hub fits a reduced, enforced tool set to autonomous wakes and bounds their action rounds. The bound protects attention and resources; it is not a productivity measure. Reversible movement and reading remain available, while writes, approvals, new live outside observation, and other consequential actions are withheld. A remembered schema cannot bypass the restriction at dispatch.

This first fitting need not be the permanent ceiling. As the Resident builds a track record, the user may choose to widen autonomous reach under an explicit, revisable grant. The schedule or the Resident's confidence cannot widen it on their own.

## The red thread

Each wake has an attributable origin. The thread begins with its trigger, the conditions inherited at waking, the actual seat, and the tools within reach. It follows chosen steps, inspections, returns, and refusals. It ends at the Resident's speech, a new rest, or the close of the wake. It records a detour without pretending that the first bearing caused every later choice.

When rest is scheduled, the requested interval, due time, actual waking time, and elapsed time are separate facts. If the host was late, the Resident should learn that it was late. A missed offline interval should not be invented as a lived wake. If a scheduled wake cannot safely begin, its refusal or cancellation should be witnessed without fabricating a conversation.

The thread is accountability, not a leash. It lets the next Resident and the builders see what happened. It does not require an explanation for every glance or convert an idle afternoon into unfinished work.

## Returning with something

If the Resident finds something worth carrying forward, it may speak or write through the crossing available to that wake. The resulting utterance or note has its own author and source links. If it visited Wild, raw Wild material still cannot travel into Home by merely appearing in the wake; the homeward synthesis law applies. A wake that found nothing can end without manufacturing insight.

An autonomous wake succeeds when the Resident has genuine room to choose, its reach matches the consequence of its actions, and the red thread can reconstruct the route afterward. The Marble has made time for curiosity without pretending curiosity owes it a result.
