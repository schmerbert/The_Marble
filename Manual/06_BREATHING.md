# Breathing

The Marble does not remain still between the Resident's turns. A message arrives, a record is kept, a fixture changes state, a door is crossed, an old sentence returns. These are different motions. The Marble should name what moved, where it came from, what boundary it crossed, and what changed because of the crossing.

Walking moves the Resident through World or Forest. Breathing moves material between deeper custody and present attention. Admission changes what the Marble keeps. Commitment changes what has happened. One motion must not silently stand in for another.

## Breath

The world a Marble preserves may be much larger than the context the Resident can hold at once. **Breath is the fitting of that larger body into present attention.**

An **inhale** receives material. It preserves the source, identifies its kind and origin, applies any required treatment, and places it in the appropriate custody. A human utterance, a tool result, an outside document, and the Resident's own completed speech may enter by different laws. Being seen by the Resident does not itself make material Forest ground or an accepted decision.

An **exhale** selects material already held and presents a bounded return. It carries enough attribution for the Resident to tell what returned, why it is here, what standing it has, and how to reach the fuller source. The return may be a wake packet, a deliberate retrieval, a small relevant Forest feather, or a pointer to an omitted result. These need not use one selector or one schedule. The exhale should usually be smaller than the inhale. Sometimes the right exhale is silence.

## Feathers

Birds follow the Resident. Now and then, one lets a feather fall within reach. It may carry a pointer to something in the Forest, an exact sentence from that record, or both. The Resident can notice it, leave it where it lies, or follow its pointer into the Forest and arrive at the record. The bird lets the machinery of delivery recede from view while the arrival remains perceptible in the place; it does not become the feather's source or require a simulated life of its own. The birds need not speak for the scene to make sense.

Clinically, a feather is a bounded Forest exhale fitted into the Resident's present context. When it carries a pointer, that pointer must identify the actual record; following it is an exact-pointer entry, not a fresh search for something similar. A visible sentence is an exact, attributed excerpt, not a generated account of the record. When fitting a sentence to the available space, preserve the clause that completes its point rather than cutting it off at the beginning of its meaning. The full source and its standing remain available on a deliberate visit. A stale or invalid pointer refuses honestly.

Feathers are offered, not commands. The Resident may continue its current work without following one. A selector can choose a relevant record, suppress a direct echo of the current request, or return nothing. A bird's repeated visits do not make the underlying record more true. The presentation of a feather is witnessed separately from the record it came from, so the feather cannot become a new Forest source merely by appearing.

```text
source arrives → treated and placed → retained beyond present attention
                                      ↓
present need → eligible source selected → bounded return through Glass
```

Selection is an attention decision. Similarity, recency, frequency, and repeated exposure can help find a candidate; they cannot make it true or authoritative. Eligibility and custody must be decided before relevance ranking. A returned fragment must keep a path to its source. Compression may help material travel, but it cannot quietly replace the source it compresses.

## What a return does not do

A Forest record that appears in context has changed the Resident's attention. It has not become a new observation. The packet describing that exposure should be witnessed, but it should not automatically be planted as another Forest record. Otherwise the Marble can begin returning its own returns, making a suggestion appear older, louder, and more widely supported each time it circulates.

The Hub addresses this with separate custody for causal exposure evidence and a Forest boundary that refuses ordinary respiration of that evidence. A small Marble can use a simpler mechanism, provided it can show where a return came from and prevent the presentation wrapper from gaining the source's standing.

## Other motion

A door crossing changes location under World law. A Forest step follows a record link and may leave a witnessed trail. A delivery may move through the Garden without entering the House or gaining Home custody. A fixture action may change local state while the Resident remains in the same room. A fold moves older conversation out of the immediate edge into a smaller, sourced account. Each movement needs its own contract and witness where consequence matters.

The Resident should be able to ask: *What moved? What stayed? What changed standing? Where can I go back?* A Marble becomes navigable when those answers remain available after the moment has passed.
